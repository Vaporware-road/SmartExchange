01  ·  Executive Summary

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Most exchange businesses still run on tools that were never designed for them. Rates live in a spreadsheet, customer requests arrive through messaging apps, banner graphics are made by hand for every price change, and banking details are re-typed from photographs. The result is predictable: slow response times, pricing that is out of date the moment it is published, avoidable transfer errors, and no reliable record of who did what.

As transaction volume grows, this model does not scale. Staff spend their day on repetitive administration instead of serving customers, and management has no consolidated view of performance, currency demand or customer value.

HOW THE PLATFORM SOLVES IT

The Exchange CRM Platform consolidates every daily operation into one connected system. A single rate table feeds the customer portal, the marketing designs, the public website widget and the API at the same moment. Customer requests arrive as structured records instead of free-text messages. Banking information is validated automatically before money moves. Every approval, status change and completed transaction is logged against a customer profile.

One source of truth, many destinations

Publish a rate once — it appears instantly in the customer portal, the Instagram banner, the Telegram post, the website widget and the public API. No duplicate entry, no version conflicts, no outdated prices in circulation.

SECTION 02
Project Overview

The Exchange CRM Platform is a comprehensive customer relationship management system built specifically for exchange businesses. Its purpose is to centralise and automate daily operations — customer management, transaction processing, exchange rate management, marketing content creation and online customer services — inside one product.

Rather than adapting a generic CRM to an industry it was not designed for, the platform is organised around the way an exchange actually works: a rate is set, a customer requests a buy or sell, the request is reviewed and verified, funds move, and the outcome is recorded and reported. Every module in the system serves a step in that chain.

Who Uses the Platform

USERWHAT THEY DO IN THE SYSTEM
CustomersRegister, submit buy and sell requests, upload documents, track status and review history through the self-service portal.
OperatorsReceive incoming requests, verify customer and banking information, process transactions and update status.
Exchange managersSet and publish rates, approve high-value transactions, oversee the daily queue and monitor performance.
Marketing staffProduce rate banners and promotional content for Instagram, Telegram and the website directly from live prices.
AccountantsReconcile completed transactions and draw revenue, volume and currency-demand reports.
AdministratorsManage users, roles, permissions, integrations and platform configuration.

The Operating Cycle

The platform closes the loop between pricing, demand and fulfilment. Rates published to the market generate customer requests; requests are verified and processed; completed transactions feed the analytics that inform the next pricing decision.

Design Principles

  • Single source of truth — one rate table drives every channel.
  • Structured over informal — every request is a record, not a message.
  • Verify before value moves — validation built into the workflow.
  • API-first — every capability is available to other systems.
  • Role-aware — staff see and do only what their role allows.
  • Built to extend — mobile, bots and partners plug into the same core.

SECTION 03
Core Features & Modules

Six functional pillars cover the full operational surface of an exchange business. Each module works independently and gains value from the others through shared data.
MODULE 01
Exchange Rate Management System

Purpose. Give the exchange one authoritative place to define, price and publish every currency it trades — with full control over how each price type is calculated and where it appears.

Business benefit Prices stay consistent across every channel and update in real time, removing the risk of trading against a stale rate and eliminating the daily work of re-publishing prices by hand.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Multi-currency support — USD, EUR, GBP, TRY, USDT and more
  • Separate buying and selling prices per currency
  • Real-time price updates across all connected services
  • Price types: cash, transfer, account-based and custom rules
  • Centralised rate management for portal, banners, widgets and API
  • Custom pricing rules for segments and special agreements

MODULE 04
Banking Information Verification System

Purpose. Validate customer banking details automatically through available banking APIs and verification services, before any funds are committed.

Business benefit Transfers to wrong or mistyped accounts are among the most costly errors an exchange can make. Automatic verification removes that class of error, strengthens compliance posture, and shortens the time an operator spends checking each request.

KEY CAPABILITIES

  • Detect bank information from card numbers
  • Retrieve card and account details automatically
  • Validate ownership information against the customer record
  • Check the accuracy of customer-provided banking data
  • Reduce human error during transaction processing
  • Process transactions only on verified information

How the six modules compound

Each module is useful alone, but the value is in the connections: rates set in Module 01 populate the banners of Module 02, the portal of Module 03 and the public API of Module 06. Requests raised in the portal are verified by Module 04 and processed by Module 05 — and every step of that chain writes back to the same customer record.
